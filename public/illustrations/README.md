# Ilustrasi login COLABORA

- `coordination-grid.png`: sumber hasil generasi, 1448 × 1086 piksel.
- `coordination-grid.webp`: aset halaman login, 127.138 byte. Dikonversi dari PNG dengan Sharp (kualitas 85), tanpa perubahan komposisi.
- Dibuat dengan tool ImageGen bawaan, bukan CLI. Ilustrasi konseptual; tidak memuat logo resmi PLN.
- Latar panel memakai token `brand-surface`; palet ilustrasi tetap terang pada kedua tema agar karya tetap terbaca.

## Prompt final

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: illustration for the left panel of the COLABORA electricity utility internal application login screen. Create one refined editorial illustration, landscape 4:3 composition, on a perfectly solid pale blue background #e3f2f5. A small Indonesian electricity distribution scene: two adult field technicians in dark teal workwear and yellow safety helmets standing safely on the ground reviewing a clipboard together, beside a distribution pole and a compact utility transformer enclosure; neatly drawn overhead wires lead toward a few simple neighborhood buildings in the distance. Mature, calm, professional flat editorial illustration with precise fine linework, simplified shapes and subtle paper texture. Palette strictly teal blue #006f85 and #00a2b9, navy #172f3b, yellow #fff200 as small accents, off-white. Clear silhouettes, accurate hands, modest detail, no exaggerated faces. Scene centered with generous clear margins, all significant subjects fully in frame; lower two thirds contain the scene and upper third remains quiet. No words, letters, labels, numbers, logos, PLN emblems, watermark, fake UI, floating icons, rounded backdrop blobs, gradients, glow, 3D rendering or photorealism. This should feel like a custom illustration for a dependable electricity operations product, not a marketing banner.
```
